Use Xcelsior for GPU compute and Headmesh for an isolated development sandbox. Add a server to your MCP client, sign in, and review its requested access.

Xcelsior

Discover GPU compute, inspect pricing, and manage your instances through scoped tools.

Streamable HTTP endpoint

https://mcp.xcelsior.ca/mcp

  1. Add this endpoint as a remote MCP server in your assistant.
  2. Complete Xcelsior's OAuth flow and review the requested scopes.
  3. Use discovery and management tools within your account's permissions. Provisioning compute requires the applicable account balance.

Headmesh

Read and edit files, run commands, manage Git and keep sessions in your own isolated development sandbox.

Your Streamable HTTP endpoint

https://YOUR_SANDBOX_HOST/mcp

  1. Deploy your sandbox and configure its public HTTPS endpoint.
  2. Configure Headmesh OAuth and explicitly grant your linked account access to that sandbox.
  3. Add your endpoint to the assistant, sign in with GitHub or Hugging Face, and approve the scopes you need.

Existing per-client sandbox bearer credentials remain supported. Keep credentials in your client's private settings.

HeadmeshOAuth setupSourceRegistry entry

Sign-in establishes your identity. Sandbox access requires a separate grant. This documentation page supplies no shared machine or credential; your assistant connects directly to the service you configure.

The Space is static and makes no model or inference calls. Each service's normal usage and account terms apply.
